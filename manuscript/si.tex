\noindent This Supporting Information documents the complete computational
output of the accompanying repository (\texttt{scale-free-framework},
release v1.0.0): the full analysis-script roster, every archived verdict
table with its frozen decision lines, and the provenance of all
third-party data. Numbers are transcribed verbatim from
\texttt{results/}. Scripts are plain Python~3
(numpy/scipy/matplotlib/pandas); most are self-contained against
\texttt{results/} and \texttt{data\_external/}, except the per-embryo
Bicoid anchor audit which requires the Nikoli\'c et al.\ repository data
(download instructions in \texttt{data\_external/PROVENANCE.md}).
